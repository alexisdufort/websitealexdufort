FAQ

Strategic partnership questions

What is a strategic partnership?

A long-term collaboration between two companies to reach new customers, add value to a product or open new channels, for example a co-branded offer, a bundle or a benefit included in another company's card or membership.

How is this different from affiliate partnerships?

Affiliate partnerships are performance-based and usually scale across many publishers. Strategic partnerships are fewer, bigger and custom: a deal designed with one partner, often with much bigger reach.

How long does a partnership take to close?

It depends on the partner's size and process. Smaller deals can close in weeks, while larger ones with banks, airlines or big networks often take several months.

Can you help after the deal is signed?

Yes. Launch, co-marketing and ongoing partner management are often where the value is won, and I stay involved through all of it.
