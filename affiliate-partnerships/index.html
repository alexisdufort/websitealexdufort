FAQ

Affiliate questions

What does a freelance affiliate manager do?

Runs your affiliate program day to day: recruiting and activating publishers, managing offers and commissions, and reporting on results, without the cost of a full-time hire.

Which affiliate networks do you work with?

GigSky's affiliate program runs on Impact, and the same principles apply on networks like Awin, CJ or PartnerStack. I can help you choose the right one for your business.

How do affiliate and earned media work together?

Many top review and best-of sites earn commissions on what they recommend. A strong affiliate program gives them a reason to feature you and keep you listed, and that coverage keeps sending traffic long after it's published.

Can you start a program from scratch?

Yes. I can help choose a network, set commissions and terms, build the first publisher list and get the first partners live.
