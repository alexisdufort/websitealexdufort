FAQ

Earned media questions

What is earned media?

Coverage you don't pay for directly: articles, reviews, best-of lists and mentions that publications choose to run. It builds trust because it comes from an independent source, and good placements keep bringing traffic for months.

What is affiliate PR?

An approach that combines editorial outreach with affiliate partnerships. Many review and best-of sites earn commissions on the products they recommend, so the right affiliate setup helps you get included and stay included.

Can you guarantee coverage?

No one can honestly guarantee editorial coverage. What I bring is the targeting, relationships and approach that make placements far more likely, and clear reporting on what lands.

How long does it take to get placements?

It depends on the outlet and your category. Some placements happen within weeks, while best-of lists update on their own schedule. I set realistic expectations after the audit.

Which industries do you work with?

Most of my experience is in travel and mobile connectivity (eSIM), with coverage across finance, tech, points and travel media. The approach works for most consumer brands.
